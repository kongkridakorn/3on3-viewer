    Mechanisms
    3, one per lid

    Each lid has its own wide push bar, spring gear and hinge pin. How it works: the bar is a rocker on a pivot peg. Two hook arms at its ends sit above small catch ribs inside the lid front wall, so the lid cannot rise. Press the bar about 1 mm and the hooks slide off the ribs, then the spring gear lifts the lid. A small compression spring in the front wall behind the bar pushes it back (drawn as a plain cylinder).
